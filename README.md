# **Academic Theses**

Benjamin Bastian-Querner — Experimental Physics, Humboldt University of Berlin

This repository provides access to my Master's thesis (2019) and PhD dissertation (2024), documenting research and technical contributions in scientific instrumentation, software development, and optical calibration.

## **Master's Thesis (2019)**

**Characterization of Cylindrical Wavelength Shifting Optical Light Guides**

Development of an automated experimental setup for characterizing optical sensors, including a five-axis robotic scanner with geometry-based motion planning and collision avoidance.

Selected technical contributions:
- Python-based robotic motion planning and measurement automation.
- Geometric collision avoidance without dedicated proximity sensors.
- Automated scanning across different positions and angles of incidence.
Relevant sections: Chapter 4 — Five Axis Scanner and Functional Demonstration; Chapter 6 — Cross Check with Different Methods.

## **PhD Dissertation (2024)**

**Calibration of Standard Stars**

Research on precision spectrophotometric calibration, including the development of automated optical measurement systems, calibration software, and data-processing pipelines.

Selected technical contributions:
- Development of automated measurement and calibration software.
- Integration of scientific instrumentation and hardware control.
- Data acquisition, calibration algorithms, and analysis pipelines.
Relevant sections: Chapter 3 — Experimental Setup; Chapter 5 — Calibration and Data Analysis.
